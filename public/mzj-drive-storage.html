MZJ Drive Storage

MZJ Drive Storage is the file-storage integration used by MZJ Platform to securely store, organize, upload, download, and manage work files associated with marketing campaigns, agendas, tasks, and delivery folders.

Purpose of the application

The application connects MZJ Platform to Google Drive so authorized MZJ Platform users can work with task and campaign files from inside the platform while Google Drive acts as the underlying storage provider.

How Google Drive is used

MZJ Drive Storage creates and manages files and folders used by the platform. Employees access those files through MZJ Platform according to their platform permissions and do not need to sign in to the company Google Drive account.

Data access

The integration uses Google Drive access for files and folders created or selected for use by the application, solely to provide storage functions requested from within MZJ Platform.
